# MILESTONE - Project Preferences

> **📅 ONE-TIME DEADLINE EXTENSION**
>
> CMSE 495 milestones are normally due on **Sunday evenings**. However, because project teams will be formed by instructions on Tuesday, this milestone may be submitted until **Monday at 11:59 PM** without penalty.
>
> No extension request is required. This is a one-time adjustment due to the team formation process and is not a general course policy.

During the first week of class, you will review project opportunities and submit your project preferences. The information collected in this assignment will be used by the instructors to form project teams.

- [Link to Microsoft Teams]
- [Link to Project Survey](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/yjb0Axfbb3)

Team formation is a balancing process that considers:

- Student interests and preferences
- Building balanced teams
- Project requirements

While we will do our best to place students on projects of interest, not everyone will receive their first-choice project. The goal is to create teams that can successfully complete their projects while providing students with a meaningful capstone experience.

Projects are proposed by a variety of community partners including industry, government, nonprofit, academic, and campus organizations. These projects differ in goals, scope, constraints, and opportunities, so different students may find different projects appealing.

## Objectives

This milestone is designed to help you:

- Explore available project opportunities
- Reflect on your goals for the capstone experience
- Gather information needed to make an informed decision
- Practice evaluating opportunities using evidence rather than assumptions
- Provide instructors with information needed to form project teams

## Step 1: Reflect on Your Goals

Before evaluating projects, consider what you hope to gain from your capstone experience.

To help with this process, review and complete:

- `Project_Goal_Reflection.md`

This worksheet is intended to help you identify your goals, priorities, interests, and constraints before evaluating project opportunities.

Different students have different definitions of success. Your goal is to identify the opportunities that best align with your personal and professional goals.

## Step 2: Review Project Descriptions

Review all project descriptions available in Microsoft Teams.

- Project descriptions will be available in the **General** channel under **Files**.
- Not all project descriptions may be available before the first day of class.
- Additional information and discussion may occur during class meetings.

Like all assignments in this course, students are expected to complete all work regardless of class attendance. All information necessary to complete this milestone will be made available through Teams and course materials.

## Step 3: Evaluate Project Opportunities

Use the project descriptions and any information gathered through class discussions and course communications to evaluate projects.

To help organize your evaluation process, complete the following worksheets:

- `Project_Selection_Matrix.md`
- `Final_Project_Review.md`

These worksheets are designed to help you organize your thoughts, compare opportunities, record questions, and develop project preferences. While these documents will not be graded, students are expected to thoughtfully engage with the process as part of preparing their project preferences.

As you review projects:

- Start with the information available.
- Ask questions when information is unclear.
- Distinguish between evidence and assumptions.
- Consider how each project aligns with your personal goals.
- Focus on project fit rather than identifying a single "best" project.

You will rarely have perfect information when making professional decisions. The goal is not to eliminate uncertainty but to make the best decision possible using the information available.

## Step 4: Submit Your Preferences

Complete the Project Preference Survey and provide ratings for each project.

Use the following scale consistently across all projects:

| Rating | Meaning |
|----------|----------|
| 5 | One of my top choices |
| 4 | Interested |
| 3 | Neutral |
| 2 | Not interested |
| 1 | Please do not assign me |

To help the instructors form teams, use the full rating scale whenever appropriate. Thoughtfully distinguishing between projects provides more useful information than assigning similar ratings to every project.

Your responses will play a significant role in the team formation process.

## Deliverable

Because project teams will be formed immediately after the deadline, late submissions may limit the instructors' ability to incorporate your preferences into the team formation process.

## Reminders

- There is no single "best" project.
- Different students have different goals, interests, and constraints.
- Your goal is to identify projects that appear to be a good fit for you based on the information available.
- Project preferences help inform team assignments but do not guarantee placement on a specific project.
- Questions should be raised as early as possible so they can be addressed before the survey deadline.
